# Illuminati privacy policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

Illuminati is a free e-reader for public-domain books. It has no accounts,
no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This policy explains what the app does
with information, on every platform it runs on (Android, Windows, macOS and
Linux).

## What stays on your device

Everything you create or choose in the app is stored only on your device:

- your library: downloaded books, covers, reading positions and reading lists
- your shelves, highlights, notes and bookmarks
- your settings, such as fonts, theme, brightness and app language

None of this is sent anywhere. It is deleted when you remove the book or the
app (on Android, uninstalling the app deletes it; on desktop it is kept in
the app's data folder until you delete it).

## What the app sends over the internet

The app connects to the internet only to do what you ask it to:

| When you… | The app contacts | What it sends |
|---|---|---|
| Browse, search or download books | Project Gutenberg (gutenberg.org), Standard Ebooks (standardebooks.org), Wikisource and Wikidata (wikisource.org, wikidata.org, ws-export.wmcloud.org), Internet Archive (archive.org) | Your search words and the books you open or download |
| Look up a word with **Define** | Wiktionary (en.wiktionary.org) | The word you selected |
| Translate a passage with **Translate** | MyMemory (api.mymemory.translated.net) | The text you selected and the language pair |
| Open the app (versions downloaded from GitHub only) | GitHub (api.github.com, github.com) | A request for the latest version, to offer updates |

Like any internet request, these services see your device's IP address and
the app's name and version. Illuminati does not send them any identifier,
account or other personal information. Each service handles requests under
its own privacy policy.

The Google Play version does not check GitHub for updates; Google Play
updates it.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect, sell or share personal information.
- It does not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.
- It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, camera,
  microphone or files.

## Children

Illuminati is a general-audience reading app and is not directed at
children. The catalogues it searches contain classic literature for all
ages, including works written for adults.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new
date.
